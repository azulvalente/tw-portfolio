# Technical writing

Technical writing is about making knowledge accessible and useful to the people who need it. That might mean helping users understand a product, helping technical teams understand a process, or capturing valuable knowledge for the company that would otherwise remain in people’s heads. It can also provide transparency into how products, processes, and systems work.

For me, this means treating documentation as a product. It should evolve alongside the product it describes, staying useful, accurate, and representative of what the product actually is. When documentation is disconnected from the product, it can quickly become outdated and difficult to trust.

I also believe documentation should have a clear purpose. Not every piece of information needs to become documentation, and not every organization has the same documentation needs. The decision to invest in documentation should come from an actual need within the product or organization. When that need exists, the Technical Writer’s role is to turn that need into documentation that provides real value.

Ultimately, good documentation part of how a product communicates with the people who use and build it.
