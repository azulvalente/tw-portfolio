# About Me

I’m a Technical Writer based in Buenos Aires, with over five years of experience creating clear, user-focused documentation for software products. I work closely with subject matter experts, developers, product teams, and other stakeholders to understand complex concepts and turn them into documentation that is useful and accessible to its intended audience. 

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Translation from the University of Buenos Aires, where I specialized in legal translation. Although legal translation and technical writing may seem like very different fields, that background taught me something that has become central to my work as a Technical Writer: how to make complex information easier to understand and approach.

Before becoming a Technical Writer, I led AI data training projects, working with clients from different countries and coordinating with internal teams and project participants throughout the project lifecycle. This gave me valuable experience working across teams and communicating with people in different roles and professional backgrounds.

I’ve also completed a course in Natural Language Processing (NLP), and I’m currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Translation and New Technologies.

I’m interested in the intersection of language, technology, and in how new technologies are changing the way we create, manage, and communicate technical information.
